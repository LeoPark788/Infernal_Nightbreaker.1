01 / The premise

A legend feared.
A person unseen.

An immortal hidden beneath centuries of myth. An elite assassin trained to destroy him. A bond that begins with suspicion—and could become the one thing powerful enough to save him from himself.

Genre

Dark fantasy

Supernatural action, romance, mystery, and psychological drama in a world of secret agencies and dangerous experiments.

Core themes

What survives forever?

Trust, immortality, loneliness, love, corruption, loyalty, and the fear of losing someone precious.

Central question

Can power protect love?

Leo must learn that protecting someone is not the same as controlling every danger around them.

02 / Character dossiers

Two dangerous worlds collide.

One is a myth the hidden world wants to control. The other is its finest weapon—and a person brave enough to question who is holding the leash.

Dossier 001

Leo Park

The Infernal Nightbreaker · Immortal

An immortal, nonhuman being whose true origin remains unknown. Criminal organizations, secret agencies, and experimenters fear him as an ancient entity—or a threat that must be captured or destroyed.

He protects the vulnerable without seeking recognition or worship. Centuries of isolation made him calm, observant, patient, and difficult to deceive. Beneath his intimidating composure is a playful, affectionate, occasionally shy man who longs for genuine connection.

Ryujin brings out a side almost nobody has seen. His greatest emotional vulnerability is the possibility of losing someone he truly loves.

Black hair · blond tipsBrown eyesShadow & lightProtective
Dossier 002

Shin Ryujin

Elite assassin · Human

The highest-ranked and strongest operative in a secret organization. Her sleek, elegant, mysterious style has an Ada Wong-inspired energy: alert, composed, and always weighing the exits.

Intelligent, analytical, independent, sarcastic, and hard to intimidate, Ryujin distrusts people who conceal information. She is guarded but loyal to those she truly trusts.

She is not simply a love interest who softens Leo. She has her own goals, judgment, abilities, and struggles—and falling in love never erases her independence.

05 / Internal threat

The corruption.

The most dangerous thing about Leo may not be the people hunting him. It may be what happens when fear becomes stronger than his control.

Origin & progression

A mark around the heart.

The corruption is tied to immense supernatural power, prolonged isolation, emotional instability, and an overwhelming fear of losing someone important.

It begins as a dark mark around Leo's heart. Dark veins spread through his body, particularly along his right side. His eyes turn jet black, his vision can blur, and his shadows behave like a black hole.

What it threatens

More power. Less control.

Corruption intensifies his abilities and aggression, distorts reality, fuels paranoia, and makes genuine threats harder to distinguish from perceived ones. His usual restraints begin to disappear.

Ryujin's voice, presence, touch, and affection can anchor him, slow the corruption, and help him regain control—but she cannot erase every consequence instantly.

His lesson: loving someone does not mean surrendering to the fear of losing them. Her lesson: standing beside him cannot mean ignoring danger or losing her agency.

08 / Continuity archive

Important canon rules.

Details that must remain true across every chapter, scene, and future version of the story.

  1. Leo is immortal and nonhuman; his exact origin remains mysterious.
  2. His natural eyes are brown; other colours correspond to abilities or emotional states.
  3. His hair is black with blond highlights at the tips.
  4. Ryujin is a formidable human assassin who remains intelligent, capable, and independent.
  5. Ryujin's aesthetic is Ada Wong-inspired; she must not become a generic love interest.
  6. Leo is calm and observant, not constantly serious or hyperactive. He becomes playful when comfortable.
  7. His softer, shyer, affectionate and clingy side appears through genuine trust; Ryujin brings it out.
  8. Corruption amplifies Leo's power while threatening his mental stability and control.
  9. Ryujin can anchor him during corruption, but consequences do not instantly disappear.
  10. Leo never cheats on Ryujin. Yuqi's interference does not change his loyalty.
  11. Ryujin keeps her independence after falling in love. Their relationship is based on mutual respect, not ownership.
  12. Leo's frightening public reputation contrasts with his private gentleness toward people he trusts.
The heart of the story
“The world fears Leo because of what he can destroy. Ryujin learns to see the person behind that power.”

At its core, The Infernal Nightbreaker is about an immortal who has spent centuries without truly belonging anywhere and a human assassin trained to destroy the very being who will change her understanding of the world. Their love does not remove every danger. It gives them a reason to keep choosing each other despite those dangers.
